# DaKnowledge Map

A working synthesis of the published site as of 2026-08-13. This is the agent's reading of the knowledge base, not a new doctrinal source.

**Center:** the Triune God, received in Catholic Scripture and Tradition.  
**Voice:** catechetical and pastoral on doctrine pages; academic on relics.  
**Scale:** about 52,000 words across 11 sections.

---

## What the site already teaches

### Theology Proper
God is one in essence, simple, and both transcendent and immanent. The Father is the unoriginate source. Incommunicable attributes (aseity, simplicity, immutability, eternity, omnipresence, omnipotence, omniscience) belong to God alone. Communicable attributes (goodness, truth, beauty, justice, mercy, love) are reflected in creatures. Names such as Elohim, Yahweh, El Shaddai, and Abba are treated as revelations of who God is, not mere labels.

### Trinity
One God in three persons. The Father begets, the Son is begotten, the Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son (Filioque). The immanent Trinity is God in himself; the economic Trinity is God toward us. Missions reveal processions without exhausting them. *Opera trinitatis ad extra sunt indivisa.* Rahner's axiom is noted as debated, not adopted as a rule.

The Trinity overview page was a stub. The person pages and the economic/immanent page already carry the doctrine.

### Christology
Jesus is one divine person with two natures, without confusion, change, division, or separation (Chalcedon). He is truly God and truly man. Titles: Christ, Son of God, Son of Man, Lord, Savior. States of humiliation and exaltation. Work: prophet, priest, and king; revelation, redemption, reconciliation, glorification. Athanasius: God became man that man might become God.

### Pneumatology
The Spirit is a divine person, not a force. He speaks, teaches, testifies, intercedes, guides, and can be grieved. He is at work in creation, Israel, the Incarnation, the life of Christ, and the Church at Pentecost. Topics cover person, work, and gifts/fruits.

### Soteriology
Salvation is by grace. Original sin wounds nature; actual sin ruptures communion. Catholic justification is transformative: God makes the sinner righteous by infused grace, not by mere forensic declaration. Faith must be living faith (faith working through love). Ordo salutis: election, calling, conversion, justification, adoption, sanctification, perseverance, glorification. Atonement is presented as a family of complementary models (satisfaction, substitution, ransom, victory). Election is sovereign and does not cancel freedom.

### Ecclesiology
The Church is the Mystical Body, temple of the Spirit, and communion of saints: human and divine, visible and spiritual. Four marks: one, holy, catholic, apostolic. Hierarchical government: pope, bishops, priests, deacons. *Extra ecclesiam nulla salus* is framed as a call to mission, not a denial of God's wider mercy.

### Sacraments
Seven efficacious signs. *Ex opere operato* for validity; *ex opere operantis* for fruitfulness. Matter and form. Baptism, Confirmation, and Eucharist initiate; Reconciliation and Anointing heal; Orders and Matrimony serve communion. The Eucharist is source and summit: real presence, transubstantiation, one sacrifice re-presented, conditions for worthy reception.

### Scripture
Inspiration is God-breathed without mechanical dictation: two authors, God and the human writer. Inerrancy is tied to what the sacred authors intended to affirm for salvation (Dei Verbum 11). Catholic canon: 73 books, including the deuterocanonicals via the Septuagint. Interpretation: one coherent whole, read in the Church, four senses (literal, allegorical, moral, anagogical), lectio divina.

The Scripture overview page was a stub. The three subpages already carry the doctrine.

### Prayer
Treasury of liturgical, personal, contemplative, and devotional prayer. Core texts: Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be, Apostles' Creed. Forms include Mass and Hours, meditation and intercession, silent contemplation, Rosary, novenas, litanies, Stations.

### Relics
This is the deepest research section. Classes of relics, veneration vs worship (*dulia* / *latria*), scriptural precedent, and long articles on the Shroud of Turin, True Cross, Crown of Thorns, Holy Lance, and Veil of Veronica / Sudarium of Oviedo. The Shroud article is uniquely academic (frontmatter, abstract, scientific debate).

### Spiritual Formation
One personal discipleship reflection from Spanish-language parish formation (2026-03-28). Themes: providence, imperfect striving, humble correction, love before doctrine, faithfulness in small things.

## Voice and depth

Doctrine pages are usually 800–1,100 words: Scripture, CCC, a Father or council, a short reflection. Relic pages are 2,000–4,300 words and source-heavy. That split is the main quality inconsistency. Optimization should either deepen the loci to match the relics' care, or keep relics as a research specialty and stop treating them as the house style for everything else.

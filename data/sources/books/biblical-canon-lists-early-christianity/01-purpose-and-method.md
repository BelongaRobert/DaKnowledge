## Claim

Early Christian canon lists are among the clearest witnesses to which books churches treated as Scripture. Collecting them in one place corrects popular oversimplifications about when and how the biblical canon formed.

## Evidence

Gallagher and Meade present the lists (usually Greek or Latin, with English) as primary evidence for what compilers considered canonical, with brief introductions and notes rather than a replacement for magisterial definition.

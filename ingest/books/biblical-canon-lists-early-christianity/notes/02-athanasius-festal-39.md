# Extract

## Claim

In the Easter letter of 367, St. Athanasius gives the earliest surviving list that names exactly the twenty-seven New Testament books later received by the whole Church. For the Old Testament he follows a twenty-two-book Hebrew reckoning as "canonical," and places Wisdom, Sirach, Esther (in some reckonings), Judith, Tobit, and similar books among those "appointed by the Fathers to be read" especially for catechumens—valued for reading, distinguished from the narrower "canon" label in his letter.

## Evidence

Primary: Athanasius, *Epistola festalis* 39. English access via standard patristic translations (e.g. NPNF / New Advent). The letter is the reason Athanasius appears on this volume's cover.

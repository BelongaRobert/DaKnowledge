## Claim

Eusebius of Caesarea (*Church History* 3.25) sorts Christian writings into books universally acknowledged (*homologoumena*), books disputed yet known to many (*antilegomena*), and books rejected as spurious or heretical. This scheme shows the canon forming by reception across churches, not by a single early decree.

## Evidence

Primary: Eusebius, *Historia ecclesiastica* 3.25. Among the acknowledged: the four Gospels, Acts, the Pauline letters, 1 John, 1 Peter. Among the disputed: James, Jude, 2 Peter, 2–3 John, Revelation (in some places). Rejected: works falsely ascribed or used by heretics.

# Extract

## Claim

The Muratorian Fragment (Latin, often dated late second century, though scholarship debates the date) is an early Western list of New Testament books. It affirms the four Gospels and many Pauline letters, mentions Luke–Acts, and excludes writings associated with heretics; its surviving text is incomplete at the beginning and differs in places from the later settled twenty-seven.

## Evidence

Primary: Muratorian Canon (fragment discovered by Ludovico Antonio Muratori). Standard editions and translations in patristic / NT introduction literature; collected with notes in Gallagher & Meade.

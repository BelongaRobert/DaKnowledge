## Claim

The North African councils of Hippo (393) and Carthage (397), in continuity with Augustine's reckoning in *On Christian Doctrine* 2.8, list the books that match the Catholic Bible's shape: the deuterocanonical books with the rest of the Old Testament, and the twenty-seven of the New. Trent (1546) later defined this received canon against Reformation disputes; it did not invent a new list.

## Evidence

Primary: Augustine, *De doctrina christiana* 2.8; canons associated with Hippo / Carthage as transmitted in Latin conciliar collections; Council of Trent, Session IV (8 April 1546). Gallagher & Meade place these Latin lists beside the Greek witnesses so the reception history is visible.

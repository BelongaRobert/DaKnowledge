# Eschatology

## The last things

Eschatology is the doctrine of the last things: death, judgment, heaven, hell, and the resurrection of the body. It is not an appendix. It is the direction of every other locus. God creates toward a end. Christ redeems toward a glory. The Spirit sanctifies toward a communion that death cannot break.

> *"I am the resurrection and the life; he who believes in me, though he die, yet shall he live."*  
> — John 11:25

---

## Already and not yet

The kingdom is present in mystery in the Church and the sacraments. It is not yet consummated. Christian hope is not optimism. It is the theological virtue that waits for what Christ has already begun.

The [Assumption](../mariology/assumption/) is a sign of that hope in one of us. The Eucharist is a pledge of future glory. Relics themselves only make sense if the body is destined for resurrection.

## The Creed's last articles

"I believe in the resurrection of the body and life everlasting." Those words close the [Apostles' Creed](../prayer/apostles-creed/). They are not a metaphor for fond memory. The same God who formed the body will raise it.

> *"For this perishable nature must put on the imperishable, and this mortal nature must put on immortality."*  
> — 1 Corinthians 15:53

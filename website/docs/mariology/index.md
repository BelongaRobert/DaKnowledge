# Mariology

## The Mother of God

Mariology is the Church's teaching about the Blessed Virgin Mary. It is not a side chapel added to the Gospel. It is Christology spoken in the language of the woman who bore the Word. What the Church says of Mary, she says in order to confess who Jesus is.

> *"And the angel said to her, 'The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you; therefore the child to be born will be called holy, the Son of God.'"*  
> — Luke 1:35

---

## Why Mary belongs in this knowledge base

DaKnowledge is organized around the Triune God. Mary is the first to receive that God in the flesh. She is Theotokos, Mother of God, because the one she conceived is the eternal Son. She is not a fourth person of the Trinity. She is the creature in whom the Incarnation becomes visible.

The Church venerates her (*hyperdulia*). She does not worship her. Worship (*latria*) belongs to God alone. The [Hail Mary](../prayer/hail-mary/) is a prayer of praise and petition, not a transfer of divinity.

## Mary in salvation history

She is daughter of Zion, the new Eve, and the first disciple. Her *fiat* ("let it be to me according to your word," Luke 1:38) is the human yes that the Son assumes. At Cana she points away from herself: "Do whatever he tells you" (John 2:5). At the cross she stands (John 19:25-27). At Pentecost she prays with the Church (Acts 1:14).

> *"Behold, I am the handmaid of the Lord; let it be to me according to your word."*  
> — Luke 1:38

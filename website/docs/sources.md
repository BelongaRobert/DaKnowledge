### Individual Councils

#### First Council of Nicaea (325)
Condemned Arianism; affirmed the divinity of Christ and the consubstantiality of the Son with the Father.

- **[Nicene Creed and Canons](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3801.htm)**

#### First Council of Constantinople (381)
Affirmed the divinity of the Holy Spirit; expanded the Nicene Creed.

- **[Documents](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/i_vatican_council/documents/vat-i_constitutio-pastor-aeternus_en.html)**  *(Note: Use Vatican general council archive for 381 docs)*

#### Council of Ephesus (431)
Condemned Nestorianism; affirmed Mary as Theotokos (Mother of God).

#### Council of Chalcedon (451)
Defined the two natures of Christ (human and divine) in one person.

#### Second Council of Constantinople (553)
Condemned the Three Chapters; further clarified Christological doctrine.

#### Third Council of Constantinople (681)
Condemned Monothelitism; affirmed two wills in Christ (human and divine).

#### Second Council of Nicaea (787)
Restored the veneration of icons; defined the difference between latria and dulia.

#### Fourth Council of Constantinople (869–870)
Deposed Photius; discussed Filioque controversy.

#### First Lateran Council (1123)
First Western ecumenical council; addressed investiture controversy and clerical discipline.

#### Second Lateran Council (1139)
Addressed simony, clerical celibacy, and military orders.

#### Third Lateran Council (1179)
Condemned Waldensians and Albigensians; required two-thirds majority for papal elections.

#### Fourth Lateran Council (1215)
Most important medieval council; defined transubstantiation, required annual confession and Easter communion.

#### First Council of Lyon (1245)
Excommunicated and deposed Emperor Frederick II.

#### Second Council of Lyon (1274)
Attempted reunion with Eastern Orthodox; defined doctrine on the procession of the Holy Spirit.

#### Council of Vienne (1311–1312)
Suppressed the Knights Templar; discussed crusades and church reform.

#### Council of Constance (1414–1418)
Ended the Western Schism; condemned Hus and Wycliffe.

#### Council of Basel/Ferrara/Florence (1431–1445)
Attempted reunion with Eastern Orthodox; discussed papal primacy and Filioque.

#### Fifth Lateran Council (1512–1517)
Last council before Protestant Reformation; addressed church reform.

#### Council of Trent (1545–1563)
The Catholic Reformation council; defined doctrine on Scripture and Tradition, sacraments, justification, and the Mass.

- **[Council of Trent — Documents](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/)**  *(Use Vatican archive)*

#### First Vatican Council (1869–1870)
Defined papal infallibility and primacy of jurisdiction.

- **[Pastor Aeternus (Dogmatic Constitution on the Church of Christ)](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/i_vatican_council/documents/vat-i_constitutio-pastor-aeternus_en.html)**

#### Second Vatican Council (1962–1965)
The most recent ecumenical council; 16 documents on liturgy, revelation, ecclesiology, ecumenism, and religious freedom.

- **[Vatican II — All Documents](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/)**

- **[Sacrosanctum Concilium (Constitution on the Sacred Liturgy)](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19631204_sacrosanctum-concilium_en.html)**

- **[Lumen Gentium (Dogmatic Constitution on the Church)](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_dogmatic_constitution_19641121_lumen-gentium_en.html)**

- **[Dei Verbum (Dogmatic Constitution on Divine Revelation)](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_dogmatic_constitution_19651118_dei-verbum_en.html)**

- **[Nostra Aetate (Declaration on the Relation of the Church to Non-Christian Religions)](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_declaration_19651028_nostra-aetate_en.html)**

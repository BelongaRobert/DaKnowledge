# Spiritual Formation

## Growth in the life of grace

Spiritual formation is the ordinary work of becoming a disciple: prayer, doctrine, the sacraments, and the duties of one's state. DaKnowledge is not only a map of teachings. It is meant to serve that work.

> *"Make every effort to live in peace with everyone and to be holy; without holiness no one will see the Lord."*  
> — Hebrews 12:14

---

## How this section relates to the rest

- Doctrine without formation becomes curiosity.
- Formation without doctrine becomes mood.
- The [sacraments](../sacraments/) are the ordinary means.
- [Prayer](../prayer/) is the breath.
- [Moral theology](../moral-theology/) is the shape of the day.

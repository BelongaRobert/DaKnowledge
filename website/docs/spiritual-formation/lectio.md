# Lectio divina

Four movements. One short text. Ten minutes is enough.

Take a verse from the page you just read, or from the [Scripture Index](../scripture/citation-index/).

1. **Lectio** — Read it aloud, twice.
2. **Meditatio** — Sit with a word that stays. Do not explain it away.
3. **Oratio** — Speak to God in that word: thanks, sorrow, ask, or offer.
4. **Contemplatio** — Be quiet. If nothing comes, the quiet is the act.

This is the same Scripture the [interpretation](../scripture/interpretation/) page teaches you to read in the Church. Lectio does not replace study. It keeps study from dying as information.

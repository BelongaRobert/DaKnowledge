# Councils and errors

A short story, not a catalog. Full documents live on [Sources](../sources/).

| When | Council | What the Church closed | What she confessed |
|------|---------|------------------------|--------------------|
| 325 | Nicaea I | Arianism (the Son is a creature) | The Son is consubstantial with the Father |
| 381 | Constantinople I | Denial of the Spirit's divinity | The Spirit is Lord and Giver of life |
| 431 | Ephesus | Nestorius (two persons; Mary not Theotokos) | One Christ; Mary is Mother of God |
| 451 | Chalcedon | Eutyches (one mixed nature) | Two natures, one person |
| 681 | Constantinople III | Monothelitism (no human will in Christ) | Two wills, divine and human |
| 787 | Nicaea II | Iconoclasm | Holy images may be venerated, not worshiped |
| 1215 | Lateran IV | Confusion about the Eucharist | Transubstantiation named |
| 1545–63 | Trent | Reformation disputes on Scripture, grace, sacraments | Scripture and Tradition; seven sacraments; justification as transformation |
| 1869–70 | Vatican I | Denial of a living magisterium | Papal primacy and infallibility, tightly defined |
| 1962–65 | Vatican II | A Church turned in on herself | Liturgy, revelation (*Dei Verbum*), the Church (*Lumen Gentium*) |

The same errors return under new names. If a page mentions a heresy, it is usually one row in this table.

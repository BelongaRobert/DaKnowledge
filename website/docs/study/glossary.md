# Glossary

Short senses as this site uses them. Search the header if you need the full page.

| Term | Sense | Page |
|------|--------|------|
| **Aseity** | God exists from Himself; He depends on nothing | [Attributes](../theology-proper/attributes-of-god/) |
| **Canon** | The Church's list of inspired books (73) | [Canon](../scripture/canon/) |
| **Consubstantial** | The Son is of one being with the Father | [Trinity](../trinity/) |
| **Dulia / latria** | Veneration of saints; worship of God alone | [Relics](../relics/) |
| **Economy** | God's work toward us in history | [Economic & Immanent](../trinity/economic-immanent/) |
| **Ex opere operato** | The sacrament gives grace by Christ's act, if valid | [Sacraments](../sacraments/) |
| **Filioque** | The Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son | [Trinity](../trinity/) |
| **Hyperdulia** | The special veneration due to Mary | [Mariology](../mariology/) |
| **Hypostasis** | Person; in Christ, one divine person | [Hypostatic Union](../christology/hypostatic-union/) |
| **Immanent Trinity** | God in Himself, apart from creation | [Economic & Immanent](../trinity/economic-immanent/) |
| **Inerrancy** | Scripture teaches without error what God willed for salvation | [Inspiration](../scripture/inspiration/) |
| **Inspiration** | God is principal author; the human writer is true author too | [Inspiration](../scripture/inspiration/) |
| **Justification** | God makes the sinner righteous by infused grace | [Justification](../soteriology/justification/) |
| **Mortal / venial sin** | Sin that destroys charity; sin that wounds it | [Virtue and Sin](../moral-theology/virtue-and-sin/) |
| **Natural law** | The good reason can know because we are made in God's image | [Law and Conscience](../moral-theology/law-and-conscience/) |
| **Original sin** | The inherited wound of Adam; not our personal acts | [Soteriology](../soteriology/) |
| **Theotokos** | Mother of God: she bore the person who is God | [Mother of God](../mariology/theotokos/) |
| **Transubstantiation** | Bread and wine become Christ's Body and Blood; appearances remain | [Eucharist](../sacraments/eucharist/) |
| **Two natures** | Christ is fully God and fully man, without mix or split | [Two Natures](../christology/two-natures/) |

# Three tracks

Pick **one**. Do not run them at once.

Each step is one sitting: the page, then the four study questions if you want them, then stop.

---

## Inquirer {#inquirer}

For someone new, returning, or in RCIA. About eight sittings.

1. [Existence of God](../theology-proper/existence-of-god/)
2. [The Trinity](../trinity/)
3. [Hypostatic Union](../christology/hypostatic-union/)
4. [Scripture](../scripture/)
5. [Baptism](../sacraments/baptism/) and [Eucharist](../sacraments/eucharist/)
6. [The Lord's Prayer](../prayer/lord's-prayer/) and [Hail Mary](../prayer/hail-mary/)
7. [Mother of God](../mariology/theotokos/)
8. [Heaven, Hell, and Purgatory](../eschatology/last-things/)

When a word is strange, use the [glossary](glossary/).

---

## Parish {#parish}

For a group that meets weekly. One page, twenty minutes of talk. Use the [discussion sheet](../spiritual-formation/discussion/).

| Week | Page |
|------|------|
| 1 | [Theology Proper](../theology-proper/) |
| 2 | [The Trinity](../trinity/) |
| 3 | [Person of Christ](../christology/person-of-christ/) |
| 4 | [Justification](../soteriology/justification/) |
| 5 | [Nature of the Church](../ecclesiology/nature-of-the-church/) |
| 6 | [Eucharist](../sacraments/eucharist/) |
| 7 | [Virtue and Sin](../moral-theology/virtue-and-sin/) |
| 8 | [Discipleship Reflection](../spiritual-formation/discipleship-reflection/) |

The leader reads the page beforehand. The group does not need the whole sidebar.

## Deeper {#deeper}

For someone who already knows the catechism outline and wants the joints.

1. [Economic and Immanent Trinity](../trinity/economic-immanent/)
2. [Two Natures](../christology/two-natures/) and [Councils](councils/)
3. [Biblical Interpretation](../scripture/interpretation/)
4. [Atonement](../soteriology/atonement/) and [Election](../soteriology/election/)
5. [Law and Conscience](../moral-theology/law-and-conscience/)
6. One relic article as a lab, not as a required faith: [Shroud of Turin](../relics/shroud-of-turin/)

Keep [Sources](../sources/) open. If a claim has no citation, do not treat it as finished.

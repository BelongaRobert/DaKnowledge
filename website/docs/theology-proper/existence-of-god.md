# Existence of God

## That God Is

Theology proper does not begin by inventing God. It begins with the confession that God is, and that this can be known. The Church teaches that the existence of God is not a private feeling. It is knowable by reason, and it is confirmed by revelation.

> *"For what can be known about God is plain to them, because God has shown it to them. Ever since the creation of the world his invisible nature, namely, his eternal power and deity, has been clearly perceived in the things that have been made."*  
> — Romans 1:20

---

## Faith and Reason

Reason can reach the truth *that* God is. Faith receives *who* God is: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. The two are not rivals. *Fides et Ratio* calls them the two wings on which the human spirit rises to the contemplation of truth.

The First Vatican Council taught that God, the beginning and end of all things, can be known with certainty from created things by the natural light of human reason. That does not make grace unnecessary. It means unbelief is not forced on us by the structure of the world.

> *"The fool says in his heart, 'There is no God.'"*  
> — Psalm 14:1

---

## Ways of Approach

St. Thomas Aquinas gathered five classical ways (*Summa Theologica*, I, q. 2, a. 3). They are not five gods and not five disconnected proofs. They are five paths from what we already know of the world to the One who is not a being among beings.

| Way | From | Toward |
|-----|------|--------|
| **Motion** | Things are moved | A first unmoved Mover |
| **Efficient cause** | Nothing causes itself | A first Cause |
| **Contingency** | Things need not exist | A necessary Being |
| **Degrees** | More and less good, true, noble | A maximum, the source of all perfection |
| **Governance** | Order and purpose in nature | An Intelligence that directs things to their end |

These ways do not replace Scripture. They clear the ground so that "I AM WHO I AM" (Exodus 3:14) is not heard as a noise.

---

## What Reason Cannot Do

Reason can know that God is, that He is one, and that He is the source of all that is. It cannot, by itself, know the Trinity, the Incarnation, or the life of grace. Those are revealed. The existence of God is the threshold; [the Trinity](../trinity/) is the house.

The Church also rejects two opposite errors: **fideism**, which treats reason as useless, and **rationalism**, which treats mystery as an embarrassment.

---

## The Name and the Question

To ask whether God exists is already to stand before the question of being. Creatures have existence. God *is* existence. That is why the attributes treated in [Attributes of God](attributes-of-god/) are not ornaments added to a deity we first invent. They name the One whose essence is to be.

> *"God said to Moses, 'I AM WHO I AM.'"*  
> — Exodus 3:14

---
